One question that is permanent in every job interview is “Introduce Yourself.” Even in social settings, the best way to kickstart a conversation is to introduce yourself. It gives a brief idea to the opposite person of who you are. Self-introductions give an insight into your personality and mindset. It is often said that the first impression is the best impression. Therefore, it is important to make a good first impression that is memorable and long-lasting. Self-introductions must be well-crafted based on the situation you are in. It must include several holistic aspects that help you make strong connections.
